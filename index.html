About SVJ Agro

Our story and values

Established in 2010, svj Agro has grown to become a leading name in agricultural product procurement and trading. With a strong commitment to quality and sustainability, we specialize in sourcing premium maize, rice, wheat, and dal directly from trusted farmers across Gujarat.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between farmers and markets, ensuring fresh, high-quality produce reaches our customers while providing fair prices to our farming partners. We believe in sustainable agriculture practices that empower rural communities and protect our environment.

Our Premium Products

Quality agro commodities for your needs

Maize

High-quality maize with excellent nutritional value, perfect for animal feed and industrial use.

  • Protein: 8-10%
  • Moisture: 12-14%
  • Available in 50kg bags

Rice

Premium rice varieties including basmati, sona masoori, and parboiled rice for diverse culinary needs.

  • Grain Length: 5-7mm
  • Broken: <5%
  • Custom packaging available

Wheat

Superior-grade wheat with high gluten content, ideal for flour milling and bakery products.

  • Protein: 11-13%
  • Moisture: <12%
  • Bulk and retail options

Pulses (Dal)

Fresh and nutritious dal varieties including toor, moong, urad, and chana with guaranteed quality.

  • Protein: 20-25%
  • Foreign Matter: <1%
  • Available polished/unpolished
