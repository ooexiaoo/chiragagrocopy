About SVJ Enterprises

Our story and values

Established in 2010, SVJ Enterprises has grown to become a leading name in agricultural product procurement and trading. With a strong commitment to quality and sustainability, we specialize in sourcing premium maize, rice, wheat, and dal directly from trusted farmers across Gujarat.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between farmers and markets, ensuring fresh, high-quality produce reaches our customers while providing fair prices to our farming partners. We believe in sustainable agriculture practices that empower rural communities and protect our environment.

Our Core Agro Commodities

Premium quality farm produce for domestic and international markets

Chana (Chickpeas)

High-protein chana available in both Kabuli and Desi varieties for wholesale markets.

  • 18-22% protein content
  • Excellent for dal and flour
  • Bulk packaging options

Maize (Corn)

Versatile yellow maize for food processing, industrial use and animal feed.

  • High starch content
  • Available in feed and food grade
  • Container load shipments

Wheat

High gluten content wheat ideal for atta, bread and bakery products.

  • 11-13% protein content
  • Excellent milling quality
  • FSSAI certified

Pulses (All Dals)

Complete range of pulses including toor, moong, urad, masoor and chana dal.

  • High protein sources
  • Polished and unpolished options
  • Custom packaging available

Jowar (Sorghum)

Nutritious gluten-free millet for health foods and animal feed.

  • Rich in iron and calcium
  • Drought-resistant crop
  • Available as grain or flour

Bajra (Pearl Millet)

Traditional Indian millet with high nutritional value and long shelf life.

  • Gluten-free grain
  • High fiber content
  • Suitable for arid regions

Rice

Multiple rice varieties suitable for different culinary traditions.

  • Various grain lengths available
  • Well-milled and sorted
  • Competitive export prices

Soybean

Protein-rich soybean for oil extraction and animal feed formulations.

  • 38-42% protein content
  • Non-GMO varieties
  • Bulk vessel loading capacity
